Geometry & Formula

Moon's angle above the horizon (what you see), t hours after moonrise: sin α = cos φ · cos(15° · (t − 6))

Flat: Moon stays at fixed height h, so to appear at angle α it must be x = h / tan α away, so d = h / sin α

Globe: d = √(D² − Rₑ² cos² α) − Rₑ sin α, D = 384,400 km, Rₑ = 6,371 km

Both: angular diameter θ = 2 · arctan(r / d)
